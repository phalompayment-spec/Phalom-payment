Never do

Agreed limits. They apply to the demo too.

  • Read bank-app push notifications as a "credit feed", even for a demo. G-RJ-01
  • Add a tourist convenience fee or a startup FX markup. G-RJ-02 · C12
  • Hold customer funds, run balances, do FX or connect directly to LAPNet. C01 · C03
  • Ask for or collect a bank PIN or OTP, including during agent-assisted onboarding. 01 §5.1
  • Treat a screenshot or a sound as proof of payment. C07
  • Promise instant payout or advances without an authorised funder under contract. C13 · G-REG-05
  • Show THB, USD or CNY reference prices on merchant materials unless counsel confirms it is allowed. G-REG-02
